# Campaign Strategy & Metrics

Full-funnel planning, channel mix, launch playbooks, and measurement.

## The Marketing Funnel

```
Awareness   → reach the right people (TOFU)
Interest    → earn attention with value
Consideration → build trust, handle objections (MOFU)
Conversion  → make the offer, reduce friction (BOFU)
Retention   → onboard, deliver value, expand
Advocacy    → referrals, reviews, word of mouth
```

Pirate metrics (AARRR): Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Referral, Revenue.

## Campaign Planning (fill the brief)

Use `templates/campaign-brief.md`. Define before writing any creative:

1. **Objective** — one primary goal, measurable (e.g. "500 trial signups in 30 days").
2. **Audience** — segment, pains, where they spend attention.
3. **Offer / message** — the core promise and value proposition.
4. **Channels** — pick 2–3 you can execute well, not all of them.
5. **Assets** — landing page, emails, ads, social, content.
6. **Budget & timeline** — spend per channel, key dates.
7. **KPIs** — leading + lagging indicators and targets.

## Channel Selection

| If your goal is… | Lead channels |
|------------------|---------------|
| Fast B2C sales | Paid social (Meta/TikTok), influencers, email |
| B2B pipeline | LinkedIn, content/SEO, email, webinars |
| Compounding organic | SEO content, YouTube, owned email list |
| Launch buzz | Waitlist + email, social, communities, PR |
| Retention/expansion | Lifecycle email, in-product, community |

Rule: **earned + owned** (content, SEO, email) compounds; **paid** is rented reach — use it to accelerate, not as the whole strategy.

## Product / Feature Launch Playbook

**Pre-launch (build anticipation)**
- Waitlist landing page + lead magnet
- Teasers on social, behind-the-scenes
- Email the list: what's coming + why it matters

**Launch (concentrate attention)**
- Announcement email + social blitz + community posts
- Launch-day offer or bonus with a deadline
- Founder/personal post telling the story

**Post-launch (sustain)**
- Case studies and testimonials
- Retarget non-converters; nurture sequence
- Recap + roadmap; turn buyers into advocates

## Goal → Metric Map

| Goal | Primary KPI | Supporting metrics |
|------|-------------|--------------------|
| Awareness | reach / impressions | CTR, engagement rate, branded search |
| Traffic | sessions | bounce rate, time on page |
| Leads | signups / MQLs | conversion rate, cost per lead (CPL) |
| Sales | revenue / orders | CVR, AOV, CAC, ROAS |
| Retention | retention / churn | NRR, repeat rate, LTV |

Healthy unit economics: **LTV : CAC ≥ 3:1**, and recover CAC in < 12 months.

## A/B Testing Discipline
1. One hypothesis, one variable at a time.
2. Decide sample size & duration **before** starting; don't peek and stop early.
3. Need statistical significance (≥ 95%) before declaring a winner.
4. Document the result — losing tests are learning too.
High-leverage things to test: headline, offer, CTA, audience, hero image, price framing.

## Reporting Cadence
- **Weekly:** leading indicators (traffic, CTR, signups, CPL) — adjust tactics.
- **Monthly:** lagging indicators (revenue, CAC, ROAS, retention) — adjust strategy.
- Tie every metric to the campaign objective; cut what doesn't move it.

## Self-Review Checklist
1. Is there **one** primary objective with a number and a deadline?
2. Are the chosen channels ones you can actually execute well?
3. Does every asset point toward the same conversion goal (message match)?
4. Are KPIs defined with targets **before** launch?
5. Do the unit economics work (LTV:CAC, payback)?
