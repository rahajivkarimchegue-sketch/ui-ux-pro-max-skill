# Editorial / Content Calendar

Plan, batch, and distribute content on a sustainable cadence.

## Planning Process

1. **Set the theme** per month/quarter tied to a campaign or launch.
2. **Map to funnel:** ~60% TOFU (awareness), ~30% MOFU (consideration), ~10% BOFU (decision).
3. **Pick pillars:** 3–5 recurring content categories you own (e.g. how-to, behind-the-scenes, customer stories, industry takes).
4. **Choose cadence you can sustain** — consistency beats volume.
5. **Batch-produce**, then schedule. Leave room for timely/reactive posts.

## Content Pillars (example for a SaaS)
| Pillar | Goal | Example |
|--------|------|---------|
| Educate | rank + help (SEO) | "How to reduce churn" guide |
| Inspire | brand affinity | customer success story |
| Product | activation | feature deep-dive, tips |
| Culture | trust / hiring | behind-the-scenes, values |
| Engage | community | polls, questions, hot takes |

## The 1 → Many Repurposing Engine
Produce one **anchor** asset (long blog, video, or webinar), then atomize:
```
Anchor (blog / video)
 ├─ Newsletter issue
 ├─ LinkedIn text post
 ├─ X/Twitter thread
 ├─ 2–3 short videos / Reels
 ├─ Carousel (key takeaways)
 └─ Q&A / community answer
```
One idea → a week of content across channels.

## Cadence Guidelines (sustainable starting points)
| Channel | Frequency |
|---------|-----------|
| Blog/SEO | 1–4 / month (quality over quantity) |
| Newsletter | weekly or biweekly |
| LinkedIn / X | 3–5 / week |
| Instagram / TikTok | 3–7 / week |
| YouTube | 1–4 / month |

## Workflow States
`Idea → Drafting → Review → Scheduled → Published → Repurposed`

## Self-Review Checklist
1. Does each piece map to a pillar **and** a funnel stage?
2. Is the cadence realistic for your capacity (no burnout)?
3. Is every anchor asset repurposed into ≥ 3 formats?
4. Is there a CTA tied to the current campaign on each piece?
5. Are you measuring what works and doubling down monthly?
